This is a small collection of my programming projects

Beauty Simulation is an attempt at simulating organic evolution as well as simple neural networks
Organisms are made of different cells which have unique functions
When they eat enough, they can reproduce
Their child has a chance to mutate, which adds, removes, or changes a cell
In order to adapt to their mutations, the organisms have simple brains which can learn to use their new features over time
Arrow Keys to move the camera
W to zoom in, S to zoom out
A to toggle the population graph
D to test the accuracy of data (output will appear on console)

Opus Magnum randomly generates and visualizes equations
It uses godot shaders to quickly render the complex math
WASD to move the camera
Up Arrow to zoom in
Down Arrow to zoom out
Shift to move faster
Space to generate a new equation
